# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
University students living on an allowance or part-time income. They open stdynt on their phone, between classes or at night, to answer one question: how much can I spend today?

## Product Purpose
stdynt is an offline-first PWA that turns a monthly allowance into a daily spending number. Success means a student knows today's number at a glance and finishes the month without running out of money.

## Positioning
A daily budget with rollover: money left unspent carries forward to the next day, and overspending carries forward as debt. The daily number is the product. The other tools support it.

## Operating Context
Opened many times a day, briefly, on a phone. Expenses get logged right after buying something.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Monthly budget, a daily budget with rollover, customizable expense categories (default: Food & Drinks, Subscription, Transport) with an interactive pie chart, browsing of past months and a 5-month trend chart, subscriptions (logging today's payment on add and recurring billing days), alerts at 80% and over 100%, loans and debts (owed and owing) with partial payments and optional installment schedules (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or custom) that flag overdue payments, savings goals, bank and e-wallet accounts with editable, back-datable transfers (including cash deposits and withdrawals), CSV/JSON export and import.
- Undo for 5 seconds after any removal. Installable PWA with home-screen shortcuts for Log expense, Accounts and Loans.
- React 19 + Vite, lucide-react icons, local storage, service worker for offline use.
- Mobile-first and responsive up to desktop; dark mode required.

## Brand Commitments
- The name is always lowercase: "stdynt".

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, user counts, or press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Today's number comes first, everything else second.
2. Logging an expense should take seconds, with one thumb.
3. Show money as it really is: rollover and debt are visible, never hidden.
4. Works offline, always.
