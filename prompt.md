# stdynt
stdynt is a mobile-first PWA made specifically for students

## features
- budget tracker  
	- monthly budget
	- daily budget (with rollover so unspent money carries over or debt carries forward)
	- expense categories (with pie chart)
	- subscription
	- over-budget alerts at 80% and above
	- loan/debt (your debt & people who have debts to you)
