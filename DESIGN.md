# Design: daylight

stdynt shows today's money as daylight in a window. The top of the day card is a dark well, and a faint tan tint topped by a thin tan line fills it from the bottom in proportion to what is left of today's money. Spend, and the level drops. The level is a liquid you can touch: a tap dents the surface where it lands and sends ripples out, and dragging sideways stirs it.

## Dials
Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2
- **Energy 2.** One accent (the tan level line and the tan money figures) on a deep green ground. The number is the largest thing on the screen and is plain cream.
- **Rhythm 2.** Regular rows and hairlines, broken only by the well and the donut.
- **Motion 2.** The level drains and fills, things rise and settle. Springs (`--spring`, `--spring-soft`, sampled into CSS `linear()`) replace stock ease curves. Nothing rotates except the small controls that turn (cog, delete X, the Log expense plus).

## Theme

stdynt has one theme, and it is dark: a deep green ground (`--ground`) with slightly lighter panels on it. This is how PRODUCT.md's "dark mode required" is met. There is no light/dark toggle. `:root` sets `color-scheme: dark`, so native fields and pickers match.

The palette is built on four colours: green #1E3B2F (panels), sage #7F8C6A (control boundaries), tan #C8B48A (money you have) and cream #F5F0E6 (text and actions). The ground, hairlines and secondary text are shades of the green and sage.

- **Tan means money you have, and nothing else:** the level in the well, positive carry and income figures, the carried pills, and the amount being typed in the sheet. It is never an action colour.
- **Actions are cream.** The primary button, Log expense, and the chosen option in a segmented control or chip are `--text` fills with `--ground` text. Links are cream and underlined. Focus rings are cream.
- Red only ever means money owed, overspent, or (on the storage slip) data that isn't being saved. It is never decoration.
- Warning orange marks the month crossing the alert threshold (80% by default) before it goes over 100%.
- Category colours are eight muted light inks (`INKS` in `App.jsx`), each at least 6:1 on the panel. Tan and red are excluded. Saves using any earlier ink set are mapped to the same hue in this one (`LEGACY_INKS`).

## Type

- **Bricolage Grotesque** (variable, weight and width axes) is the only family, self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable`, so it works offline.
- **Why this face:** its slightly uneven, hand-cut shapes keep a money app from reading like a bank, which suits a student's own notebook of spending. Its width axis lets one family be both the interface and the narrow figures, so nothing else has to load.
- At normal width it is the interface. At 88% width and weight 700 (`--font-stamp` plus `font-stretch: var(--stamp-stretch)`) it sets the big number, figures, dates and headings.
- Every number uses tabular figures (set on `body`), so rolling numbers don't shift.
- Headings use `text-wrap: balance` and paragraphs `text-wrap: pretty`.
- Labels are mixed case. Only the short ledger dates ("10 NOV") are uppercase.

## Signature: the level

- The well (`.well`) is the top of the day card: label top left, date top right, the number bottom left at up to 6rem.
- `--level` (0 to 1) is what is left of today's pool: available divided by available plus spent. A `::before` (`--sun` mixed 13% into the well) is scaled to it from the bottom, and a 2px `--sun` line (`::after`) rides its top edge. The level stops below the label row (`--well-head`), so it never crosses the label or date.
- The label and number are cream and the date is secondary text, on both the well and the tint, so the level can cross the number at any height.
- **Debt empties the well:** the level is at zero, the number turns red and a red line closes the bottom edge.
- Logging, editing, deleting or undoing an expense moves the level (1.1s spring) while the number rolls to its new value (`useTweened`) and dips once (`press`). On first draw the level rises from empty (`@starting-style`).

## The day card

- The well is the head of the card. The month and its budget are named once, on the month line under the card, and the daily rate is a figure on the card.
- Under the well: Daily, Carried (signed, red when negative), Income (only on days with income), Spent. With four figures and a card narrower than 540px (phones, and the desktop's two-column Today), they go two by two (a container query on `.today`), so none is cut off. Income is logged from the Log expense sheet (its Income switch): it adds to that day and raises the month's limit. Before a budget is set, the card asks for one instead.
- The card turns over at midnight: today's date is kept in state and refreshed on a timer set for the next midnight and whenever the app comes back to the foreground. Log expense always reads the live date.
- The ledger lists today's entries first (clock time, tap to edit, X to delete), then up to seven past days with what each spent and a pill showing what it carried (tan outline, red when it carried debt).
- Today's rows have no column head: a time, a name and an amount explain themselves. Past days, which carry two figures, get an "Earlier · Spent · Carried" head, so the Carried column is always labelled.
- That head is a button that folds the past days away ("Earlier" with a chevron; the Spent and Carried labels show while it is open). History lists the same days, so the days start folded at every width. The choice is remembered (`prefs.earlierOpen`). The days stay on the card because it is the only place that shows what each day carried.
- Changing the daily budget restarts the rollover that day and freezes the carry into `rolloverBase`. That carry shows as a last "BEFORE · [date], old daily rate" row, so the carried amount always has a line explaining it.
- "First day. Whatever you don't spend carries to tomorrow." only appears when nothing has been logged and nothing is carried.
- Below the card: the month line ("$X of $Y", or "$X spent" with no monthly budget), a bar that fills from empty on first draw (`@starting-style`) with a tick at the alert threshold, and with a monthly budget a single line under it: "$X left · $Y a day". Subscriptions and income are already in the totals, so they are not repeated there.

## Motion

- Crossing an entry off draws a 2px strike, then the row blurs and collapses (`.row-strike`). A new or edited line is written in with a brief tan wash (`.row-fresh`).
- Changing view uses the View Transitions API: the old pane slips out, the new one is revealed from its leading edge (`pane-in` / `pane-out`, direction-aware). The active nav mark (`.nav-ink`) has its own transition name, so the browser morphs it between tabs.
- History rows rise into view as they scroll in (scroll-driven `animation-timeline: view()`), where supported.
- The donut draws slice by slice. Hovering or selecting a slice thickens it with a spring and dims the rest, and the center reading fades in. The slice casts no shadow: thickness alone marks it. Legend rows wipe in. In the legend, only the dots of the other rows dim; their text stays at full strength, since they are still buttons.
- The alert slip unrolls from its top edge (clip-path). Toasts are pulled down from the top of the screen on phones and come up from below on wider screens.
- The sheet rises from the bottom on phones and settles into the centre on wider screens. Its fields ink in one after another. They fade and rise only: a blur on every field was dropped because animating a filter on form fields costs frames on phones.
- On first load, Log expense and the nav rise into place once (`dock-in`, `nav-in`), so the two fixed controls read as sitting above the page, not as part of it.
- Small controls respond physically: the delete X turns a quarter, the Log expense plus turns, the settings cog turns.
- The well's level is a liquid (`LiquidWell`, `src/liquid.js`): a canvas draws the same tint and line. A tap pushes the surface down under the finger and the dent spreads as ripples that settle on a spring; a sideways drag stirs it along its path. Vertical drags still scroll and a pinch still zooms (`touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom`). The surface never rises more than 12px above the level line at full, so a stir stays under the label and date. The figure stays selectable; text selection is held off only while a stir is under way. The loop stops once the liquid is at rest or the well is off screen. Under reduced motion the flat CSS level stays, and the setting is followed live: switching it on removes the canvas, switching it off brings it back.
- Panels that appear in place (add forms, loan panels, History day rows, the Earlier days, the Saved note, the date fields in the sheet) settle in on a spring from 8px above, using `@starting-style`. The History filters grow open with `interpolate-size` where the browser supports it and fade elsewhere. The filter dot pops in.
- The segmented controls' cream fill slides to the chosen option (CSS anchor positioning), and snaps where anchors are unsupported. The confirm slip fades and shrinks away on close (`allow-discrete`), faster than it arrives.
- Changing month in Categories slides the donut, legend and list the way panes slide, in the direction of travel (view transition `month-body`). The trend bars rise from empty, 50ms apart.
- **Reduced motion turns off every animation**, including the transitions, the liquid, the view transitions, the number roll, and the scroll-driven row filing. Toasts get no animation at all under reduced motion, because their exit fade is a delayed animation that would otherwise finish at once and hide them; they simply appear and are removed by the timer. The scroll-driven rule is only declared under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, because scroll timelines ignore the duration reset.

## The glass header

The header is solid ground at the top of the page. Once content scrolls under it, it turns to tinted glass: `--glass-tint` (deep ground at 84%) over `--glass-filter` (28px blur, saturation 1.12), with fine grain, a hairline and a soft shadow where it meets the page.

- **Why:** the header sits over the card the student is reading. The glass keeps the wordmark, date and settings readable while still showing the card sliding beneath, so it's clear there is more above. The tint is the ground itself, so it stays in the palette. This is an owner decision (antislop audit-004 #8).
- The glass lives on `.topbar::before` and only its opacity fades in. The blur radius never animates, because that repaints the whole backdrop on every frame while the page scrolls.
- **One material, two bars (owner decision, 2026-11-15, "make the glass feel premium"):** glass is only the header and the bottom nav (under 1000px; the desktop rail is static), the two bars that sit over moving content. Both use the same tokens (`--glass-tint`, `--glass-filter`, `--grain`) and a lit top or bottom rim (`--rim`). The nav's fade above it is a 24px ramp that ends clear, so it doesn't lay a second tint over the glass.
- **Solid floats:** the sheet, its pinned footer, the confirm slip and toasts are solid panel with the same lit rim (`--rim`, `--rim-soft`), a faint sheen, grain and the layered `--shadow-float`. They were glass before; a blur over one flat green read as murky, and the sheet's footer blurred the sheet's own content. The sheet's backdrop dims (55%) with a light 6px blur, so the page drops out of focus behind it. Log expense stays solid cream as the one action, with a lit top edge and a layered shadow that shrinks while pressed.
- Under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` the header and nav go back to solid ground with no blur. The solid floats and their rims are unchanged, and the sheet's backdrop loses its blur.

## Structure

- **Header:** the wordmark (Back on Settings), today's date, and settings. On Settings the cog is left out, since it would only link to the page already open. On Today, once a daily budget is set, the well shows the date, so the header's date is kept for screen readers only.
- **Routing:** every view has a hash address (`#/today`, `#/categories`, `#/subs`, `#/loans`, `#/accounts`, `#/settings`), so refresh, bookmarks and the back button keep the student's place. The bottom nav, the settings cog, Back and "Set up budget" are real `<a href>` links, with `aria-current="page"` on the current view.
- **Money:** a bottom nav for Today, Categories, Subs, Loans and Accounts, plus a fixed "Log expense" button that opens a sheet (native `<dialog>`). The sheet has amount, category chips (with an "Edit categories" link to Settings), account chips when accounts exist (the first is "Cash", which saves the entry with no account; the Paid from dropdowns on Subs and Loans use the same word, and a row saved that way reads "Cash" where others name their account), note, date, and an optional 24-hour time. Tapping a ledger or All expenses row reopens the same sheet as "Edit expense". Entries show their clock time while their day is current and become a date once it passes.
- **Categories view:** "Where it went", a donut of this month's spending with a legend. Tapping a slice or legend row selects that category and lists its expenses. Tap again to clear. When subscriptions are counted in the month but not logged as expenses, a line under the total says so ("Plus $6.98 in subscriptions, counted in the month total"), so this total and Today's month line can be reconciled.
- **Subscriptions, Loans and Accounts:** the list comes first. The add form opens from the add control under the list ("Add subscription", "Add loan", "Add account"), with Cancel beside Add, and closes again once the row is added. While the list is empty the form is already open.
- **The add control:** a 48px round outline icon with its label beside it (`.add-row`, `.add-dot`). It is the one way to add something: Add subscription, Add loan, Add account, and Transfer (with the transfer arrows in the circle). The circle is an owner decision (audit-009). The label stays because a bare + would sit next to the folded Log expense +, the same glyph for a different action. It is an outline so Log expense stays the one filled action on the screen.
- **Installments:** a third choice in the add-loan form, beside "You owe" and "Owed to you". It takes what it is, the total, the number of months, an optional due day and the account. Adding one logs nothing, since the student received an item and not cash. Its row shows "You owe" in red like any other debt, and the schedule on its own line under the row in secondary text ("Installment · ₱3,000.00/mo · 0 of 12 paid · due 28th · GCash"), because only the owing is a debt and the line is too long to share the row with the amount on a phone. Each payment is an expense. With a due day it is logged every month on that day, and a payment made by hand that covers the monthly amount stands in for the next due day. The last payment takes up the rounding and closes the row. The hint explaining this shows only while the form is set to Installment.
- **Figures that don't fit:** the figures under the well, the Loans and Accounts totals and the History day totals shorten until they fit their box (₱11.38M, then ₱11.4M, then ₱11M) and go back to exact when there is room (`MoneyFit`). They never just drop the cents, because a figure like ₱11,381,679 would pass for exact. Under a thousand there is no short form, so the cents go. The number in the well shortens past 13 characters. Screen readers always get the full figure. Adding a subscription logs today's payment as an expense right away (since you pay when buying a subscription) and sets `lastLogged` to today, then logs future recurring payments on its billing day. In Accounts, adding or editing an account offers presets for Bank (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Landbank, PNB, UnionBank, China Bank, Maya Bank, GoTyme Bank, Maribank) and E-wallet (Maya, GCash), plus a Custom option for any other name. A summary at the top shows Total balance, Bank and E-wallet; beneath the accounts list sits a ruled-off Transfers section to move money between accounts (which never counts as spending).
- **Categories:** stored in data (`categories: [{id, name, color}]`), default Food & Drinks, Subscription, Transport. Managed in Settings: add, rename (renames matching expenses), recolour by cycling inks, remove. At least one category always remains. The list has Ink / Name / Used column heads, and each name sits on a dashed write-on line (`--control-border`, 3.8:1), so it reads as editable without hover.
- **Removal:** deleting an expense, removing a subscription or category, and settling a loan all strike the row out and offer Undo in a toast for 5 seconds (held open while hovered or focused). None of them ask for confirmation first, with one exception: a category that expenses still use opens a "Move its N expenses to [category]" row in place, with "Move and remove" and "Keep [name]", so no expense is left with a label that isn't in the list. The toast says what happened, for example "Removed Transport. 3 expenses moved to Food & Drinks." A notice that arrives while Undo is open waits behind it and gets its full 4 seconds afterwards. Edits (entries, subscriptions, loans) and a restored backup offer the same Undo.
- **Settings:** budget (currency, monthly and daily budget up front; weekdays only, month start day, carry at the start of each month and alert threshold behind a "More budget options" fold that summarises their values while closed, open by default from 1000px and remembered in `prefs.budgetMoreOpen`; a rejected alert threshold opens the fold first), categories, and data (back up, restore, CSV, delete all). There is no Save button: text fields save when you leave them, choices when picked, and "Saved" shows beside the Budget heading for 2 seconds. A field that can't be read is pointed out on the field and nothing saves until it's fixed. The form remounts only after a restore or delete all. Under the daily budget, a suggestion ("$X a day lasts until 24 Nov. Use it") fills the field in one tap.
- **Restore:** asks first ("Restore the backup from 1 Nov? Its 98 entries replace the 120 on this phone.") unless there is nothing to lose, then offers Undo. Rows the backup holds that can't be read are left out and counted in the toast.
- **Confirm slip:** Restore, Delete all data and Erase and start over ask on a small panel (a native `<dialog>`, 16px radius, one shadow) instead of the browser's own box. The backdrop dims without blur. The slip itself is solid panel with a lit rim. The safe answer comes first and takes focus; Escape and the backdrop both mean no. Delete uses the red outline button, Restore the cream one, since it can be undone.
- **Limits:** an amount stops at 999,999,999, a note at 80 characters, a subscription or person's name at 40, a category at 24. Over the amount limit, the field says so.
- **Budget setup:** with only a monthly budget, the setup prompt offers "Use $X a day" (cream) beside "Choose my own" (outline, to Settings). The prompt is written straight on the card, with no box of its own.
- **Sheet on a phone:** the sheet scrolls and Log expense is pinned in a footer (`.sheet-foot`, a hairline above it, no gradient), so it stays in reach above the keyboard. Date and time fold into one line ("Today, 14:32 · Change"); the fields open when it is tapped, when editing an entry from another day, or when a date reason (closed month, before the budget) needs its hint. The fields stay in the form either way. Paid from chips scroll sideways in one row, with the chosen account scrolled into view.
- **Sheet extras:** a "Recent" row of up to four past entries, as 12px-cornered slips (field-coloured, the amount in tan) so they never read as the pill category chips; it scrolls sideways rather than wrapping. Hints sit under their field in plain ink-2: a date the daily number won't count, and a note that matches a tracked subscription. The note field suggests past notes through a native datalist.
- **History day heads:** the day, its totals and a chevron. The entry count is for screen readers only.
- **History search:** a search field (labelled for screen readers; the icon and placeholder say what it is) with a 44px filters button beside it. The button opens the "Show" filter and the sort switch (remembered in `prefs.historyToolsOpen`) and carries a cream dot while either is changed. From 1000px both are always shown and the button is gone. The fields are on the ground: deep-ground fields with `--control-border` boundaries (4.08:1, the same sage as every other field) and the cream focus ring. The sort switch's ring is the same sage. The old 339px stacking rule is gone, since the search row no longer shares its width. While a filter is on, a count line with "Clear" appears, and matching days start open.
- **Editing rows on cards:** subscription and loan rows are buttons; a subscription opens into an inline form, and a loan opens a panel under its row: record a payment, the Schedule (the next four installments, "Show all 12"), the latest three Payments ("Show all 4"), then "Edit loan details" folded away, and Close. Settle stays on the row.
- **Storage errors:** if saved data can't be read, a persistent red slip says nothing is being saved and offers "Restore backup" and "Erase and start over" (never "Start fresh", which is the monthly carry setting). Saving resumes once either succeeds. If only some rows can't be read, the rest load, a toast says how many were left out, and the save as it was is kept aside.
- **Crash screen:** a panel with "Save a copy of my data" (cream, shown when there is a save) and "Reload stdynt". The copy is a file Restore can read.
- **Layout:** phone-first single column. From 640px the column widens, the donut sits beside its legend, and the sheet becomes a centred dialog. From 1000px a sticky side rail holds Log expense and the nav, Today splits into card and History columns (the card gets the larger share), and Settings into two columns.
- **Phones on their side:** under 1000px wide, in landscape, and under 520px tall, the nav drops to 48px with each icon beside its label (as in the desktop rail), Log expense is always the folded round button, and the header scrolls with the page, because the three together covered two thirds of a 360px-tall screen. Settings keeps its header pinned, since Back is its only way out. The rule is keyed on landscape so the on-screen keyboard, which also shortens a portrait screen, doesn't trigger it. Page padding, the nav and the folded button respect the left and right safe areas, where the notch and home indicator sit in landscape.
- **Phones (under 640px):** the Log expense bar folds into a 56px round button at the bottom right while scrolling down, and opens again on the way up or near the top. The sheet has a grip and can be pulled down by its header to close (more than 110px, or a quick flick). The figures under the well shrink with the screen, and four of them go two by two, so none is cut off at 320px. A wrapped Add button spans the full width. Number fields show no spinner arrows. Under 340px of width (and in the desktop Today column near 1000px) the History search and its Show filter stack, so neither is cut off.
- **History rows:** one line (name, then category and time) when History is 480px or wider. Narrower (phones, the desktop Today column) the category and time go under the name, so the name is never cut off first. A container query on `.history` does this.
- **Touch:** every hover style sits behind `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so none of them sticks after a tap, and a stylus or a phone that wrongly claims hover doesn't get them either. Rows, buttons and text links get `:active` states instead, so there is always an answer under the thumb. Controls use `touch-action: manipulation` and aren't text-selectable. Saving and deleting call `navigator.vibrate` where it exists (one short pulse), and an error notice gives a double pulse so it feels different from a save. `overscroll-behavior-y: contain` on the page stops a pull at the top from reloading the installed app while keeping the native edge bounce.
- **Install:** `index.html` carries iOS standalone meta tags and `interactive-widget=resizes-content`, so the keyboard resizes the page instead of covering the sheet. The manifest starts at `#/today` and has a "Log expense" shortcut (`?log=1`) that opens the sheet on launch. Two more shortcuts open Accounts and Loans. Settings has an Install card: where the browser can install (Android, desktop Chromium) it is an outline "Install stdynt" button with a phone icon, so it doesn't share the download arrow with Back up and Export CSV; on an iPhone, which has no install prompt, it is the two steps (Share, then Add to Home Screen) with "Not now" to put it away for good on that browser. The card is absent once installed. iOS launch images (`public/splash/`, twelve portrait iPhone sizes) show the app icon on the ground colour, so an installed app opens on the ground instead of a blank flash.
- **Offline:** every file of a build is stored the first time the app is opened online, and the app always opens from that copy, never from the network (`public/sw.js`; the file list is written at build time). A new build downloads in the background and takes over the next time the app goes to the background, but not while a sheet is open, an Undo is showing, or a text field holds unsaved typing.
- **Saving files:** while Back up or CSV waits on the share sheet, its button reads "Saving…", is disabled and has `aria-busy`.
- **Shapes:** controls are pills (buttons, chips, segmented controls, the carried pills, Log expense). Fields and small slips are 12 to 14px. Cards and dialogs are 16 to 20px. The well is square inside the card's corners.
- **Folds:** every disclosure on a card (`Fold` in `App.jsx`: More budget options, Edit loan details) is one full-width 44px row with a hairline above, a label, an optional one-line summary while closed, and a chevron that turns. The folded fields stay mounted and `hidden`, so forms read them unchanged.
- **Cards:** content sits on flat panels with a 16px radius and no shadow. Lists on the ground use hairline rules. Only things that float (Log expense, the sheet, the confirm slip, toasts) cast a shadow. On phones a short ground-to-clear fade sits above the bottom nav (`.bottom-nav::before`), so rows scrolling under Log expense fade out instead of being cut by it. It is the only gradient. Under glass it is shortened to 24px and ends mostly clear, so it doesn't stack a second tint on the nav.

## Icons

- `lucide-react`, as PRODUCT.md requires. Stroke weights are 2 to 2.5, tinted from the palette (cream and secondary text), and icons are `aria-hidden` next to a visible or `aria-label` name.
- **App icon:** the well. Deep ground, the tan tint filled to the tan level line, and the lowercase wordmark in cream where the number sits. The maskable versions keep the wordmark inside the middle 80%.

## Rules

- All controls are native: buttons for actions, links for navigation, radio chips, inputs, and a native dialog. The design supplies type, palette, density and the level, never the controls.
- Tap targets are at least 44px, and the main actions are 56px.
- Focus is always visible: a cream ring on every surface. Ledger and History rows draw their ring inside the row. The big amount field draws its ring on its wrapper (`:focus-within`). A focused donut slice gets its own cream arc just outside the ring, so keyboard focus never looks like selection (a thicker slice).
- Live messages (the alert slip and toasts) render inside always-mounted `role="status"` regions, so screen readers announce them.
